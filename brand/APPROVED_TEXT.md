## How customers order (two ways only)
1. Send their own print file (STL / 3MF or a model link).
2. Pick one of our existing pieces.

We print it in 3D in the color they choose. We do not design from ideas or photos, and we never use "print on demand" / "طباعة حسب الطلب".
