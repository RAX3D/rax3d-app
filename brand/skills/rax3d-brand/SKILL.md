# RAX3D Brand Identity

RAX3D is a 3D printing shop in Dammam, Saudi Arabia. Instagram: @rax3d.sa. Niches: Coffee, Automotive, Tech (plus home, kitchen, garden, camping, sea, BBQ, bedrooms, bathrooms, bikes, construction tools, gifts, parties, seasons). Printed on a Bambu Lab P2S with AMS, so pieces come in any color.

**Positioning (since Oct 2026): we print, we don't design.** A customer orders in exactly one of two ways: (1) **sends their own print file** (STL/3MF or a model link), or (2) **picks one of our existing pieces**. RAX3D prints it in 3D in the color they choose. Never promise to design from an idea, sketch or photo, and never describe the business as "print on demand" / "طباعة حسب الطلب".

Apply every rule below to every RAX3D output. If a request conflicts with these rules, follow the user's request for that piece and mention the conflict in one line.

## Name & logo
- Brand name is always written **RAX3D** (all caps, no space). Handle: **@rax3d.sa**.
- The logo is a 3D project and its color changes from time to time — it is no longer gold/black. Never assume a fixed logo color; use the latest logo file the user provided. If none is available for the current piece, ask for it or use the RAX3D wordmark neutrally.
- On backgrounds use the RAX3D wordmark, not the old big "X" mark.

## Fixed slogan (all ads and promo videos end with it, in English)
> Order Your Piece – We Print It In 3D, In The Color You Choose
> DM us on Instagram @rax3d.sa

Arabic version (captions/voiceover): **اطلب قطعتك ونطبعها لك 3D باللون اللي تختاره**

Retired slogan — never use again: ~~Turn Your Idea Into 3D~~ (and any "نصمم فكرتك" wording).

## Visual style
- Base look: light, soft "watery" (مائية) background with the RAX3D wordmark. Avoid dark, heavy backgrounds.
- Every design uses a **category-themed background** matching the product's category (coffee, car, home, kitchen, garden, camping, sea, BBQ, bedroom, bathroom, Harley, racing bikes, desert bikes/ATVs, bicycles, construction tools, giveaways, gifts, parties, and seasons: Ramadan, Eid al-Fitr, Eid al-Adha, National Day, Founding Day, back to school, summer). Generate these in Canva when possible.
- Product photos arrive on a white background: cut the product out cleanly and place it on the themed background with realistic shadow.
- Add category props around the product: scattered coffee beans for coffee tools, backlight for decor, chips/crumbs for a chips holder, keys/hook for keychains, etc. Props support the product, never cover it.
- Clean and premium; avoid generic "AI-looking" layouts (purple gradients, cards-on-cards, filler text).

## Standard set for every new product
1. **Post** (feed): product on its category-themed background.
2. **Story** (9:16): animated "spotlight" video with English text: **NEW ARRIVAL / <design name> / DM TO ORDER**, on the matching themed background, with music (preferred styles: energetic, or Arabic oud/Khaleeji rhythm).
3. **"New post" promo video** featuring that post, published as a story after it.
- Default scheduling: 1:00 pm Riyadh time.

## Captions
- Arabic + English.
- Clear call to action to DM to order.
- Always mention the piece can be printed in different colors.
- Exactly 5 hashtags.
- **Never mention prices** anywhere public — pricing is handled privately in DMs.

## Voice
- Friendly, confident, Gulf-friendly Arabic; short punchy English.
- Focus on: "your piece, your color" — order it, we print it.

## Briefing a designer or another tool
When writing a brief for the motion designer or any external tool, include: the slogan end card, the themed-background rule, no prices, and the latest logo file.
